## The Rules

1. **Content does not stop.** Not for one week. Not for app launches, not for B2B deals, not for Ramadan. The content engine is the growth engine. Kill it and everything downstream dies.

2. **Never post something you haven't verified.** The research agent exists for a reason. One wrong mosque address and the brand is done.

3. **Never compromise the voice.** No "Top 10 Halal Restaurants!" listicle energy. No emojis. No exclamation marks. The brand is quiet confidence. The moment it sounds like every other Muslim app, it's over.

4. **Free users are not freeloaders.** They are your distribution. Every free user who shares a city guide is marketing you didn't pay for. The free tier has to be genuinely good.

5. **B2B is the real money.** Subscriptions get you to $400K. B2B gets you to $1M. Marketplace and affiliate fill the gaps. Never lose sight of this.

6. **You are the brand until you're not.** For the first 12 months, Rihlah's credibility comes from your taste, your travel, your voice. You cannot delegate this. After 12 months, the community starts to carry it.

7. **Ship weekly.** Every week, something new goes live — a city guide, a feature, a blog post, an AI improvement. Momentum is the product.

8. **Measure what matters.** Instagram followers are vanity. Email subscribers are intent. Paying members are validation. B2B deals are the business.

9. **Stay lean.** No employees until $500K ARR. Contractors only. The moment you hire full-time, your burn rate forces you to raise money, and then you're building someone else's company.

10. **Client work comes first until it doesn't.** Vault pays the bills. Rihlah is built in the margins until it can pay its own way. Do not quit your job until Rihlah MRR covers your expenses for 3 consecutive months.
