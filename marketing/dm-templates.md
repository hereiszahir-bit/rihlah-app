## General Rules

1. **Never say "collab" or "partnership opportunity."** Just talk like a person.
2. **Lead with something specific you admire about their work.** Not "love your content" — name the actual piece.
3. **Explain Rihlah in one sentence, not a pitch deck.** They can visit the site if they want more.
4. **Make the ask concrete.** "Would you write a food essay" > "would love to explore synergies."
5. **Offer payment.** Serious creators don't work for exposure.
6. **Keep it under 200 words.** Respect their time.
7. **Follow up once after 5-7 days if no response.** Then move on.
