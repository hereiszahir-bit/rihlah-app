# Rihlah — Marketing Strategy

> Source of truth for how Rihlah builds audience, creates content, and converts attention into community.
> Updated: 2026-09-18

---

## Where We Are

Phase 1: Brand-building. Zero Instagram followers. App is live but not promoted. Blog content exists (4 posts). No newsletter. No partnerships. No ad spend.

The goal of Phase 1 is not revenue. It is trust. Every piece of content, every post, every interaction is building the asset that makes everything else work: a brand that the right people recognize as theirs.

---

## The Funnel

```
Instagram / TikTok (discovery)
  -> rihlah.io (credibility)
    -> Blog / City Guides (trust)
      -> Newsletter signup (relationship)
        -> App signup / Apply (conversion)
          -> Community (retention)
            -> Referrals (growth loop)
```

Every piece of content should move someone one step down this funnel. Not every piece needs to convert — most should just build trust.

## Content Pillars

Four recurring themes. Every post falls into one of these:

### 1. The City
Opinionated, specific destination content. Not "Top 10 Things to Do in Istanbul." More like "The one street in Kadikoy you'll think about for months." City guides, neighborhood breakdowns, under-the-radar spots.

*Format:* Carousels (city guides), Reels (walk-throughs, food close-ups), Blog posts
*Frequency:* 2x/week

### 2. The Table
Food as the universal connector. Restaurant recommendations, street food finds, the meal that changed a trip. This pillar does double duty — it's content AND it's where affiliate/marketplace revenue lives.

*Format:* Reels (eating, plating, market walks), single-image posts, Stories
*Frequency:* 1-2x/week

### 3. The People
Real travelers, real stories. Member spotlights (once we have members), founder perspective, "who's going where" updates. This pillar builds the community identity — people join because they want to be around these people.

*Format:* Carousels (Q&A style), Reels (talking head, day-in-the-life), Stories
*Frequency:* 1x/week

### 4. The Detail
Specific, practical, insider knowledge. Prayer time logistics in a new city. How to find halal food in Tokyo without an app. The one phrase in Turkish that changes how people treat you. This is the content that gets saved and shared — high utility, high trust.

*Format:* Carousels (tip lists), Reels (quick tips), Blog posts
*Frequency:* 1x/week

### 5. The Spark (5% of content)
Provocative, opinion-driven posts that start conversations. The algorithm loves debate.

- "Unpopular opinion: Dubai is not a travel destination. It's a mall with a passport stamp."
- "The best Muslim travel destination in Europe is not Istanbul. It's Sarajevo."
- "You don't need a travel bucket list. You need a friend in the city."

*Format:* Single image or text card, strong caption
*Frequency:* 1x every 2 weeks

### Balance Rule
80% editorial/value content (pillars 1-5), 20% community/product content. Never more than 1 in 5 posts should be about Rihlah itself. 20 posts of pure value before 1 post about the product.

## Instagram Strategy

### Account Setup
- **Handle:** @rihlah.io (or @rihlah if available)
- **Bio:** "Your circle, but in every city. A community for travelers who move with intention. Apply below."
- **Link:** rihlah.io (Later Linkin.bio when we scale, for now direct link)
- **Profile photo:** R. mark (cream on dark)
- **Highlights:** Cities | Food | The Community | Apply

### Posting Cadence

**Weeks 1-2: Plant the flag (6-8 posts)**
No product talk. Pure brand energy. Establish the aesthetic, the voice, the vibe.

| Post | Type | Content |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Carousel (4 slides) | Brand intro — "Your circle, but in every city" |
| 2 | Reel | City walk or food clip (Istanbul, CDMX, or Sarajevo) |
| 3 | Carousel (5 slides) | "Seven Signs You Belong Here" (blog promo) |
| 4 | Single image | Strong travel photo + short caption |
| 5 | Reel | Quick travel tip or airport moment |
| 6 | Carousel (5 slides) | "The Airport Test" (blog promo) |
| 7 | Single image | Food close-up + specific recommendation |
| 8 | Carousel (6 slides) | "Five Cities Nobody Talks About" (blog promo) |

**Weeks 3-4: Build rhythm (4-5 posts/week)**
Mix content pillars. Start introducing the community angle. First mention of the app.

- 2 carousels/week (city content, tips, blog promos)
- 2 Reels/week (food, walks, quick tips, talking head)
- 1 single image or quote card
- Daily Stories (behind-the-scenes, polls, travel questions)

**Month 2+: Optimize and scale**
- Analyze what performs (saves and shares > likes)
- Double down on top-performing formats
- Introduce "Apply to join" CTAs naturally
- Start collaboration and partnership outreach

### Engagement Strategy (More Important Than Posting)
Instagram rewards accounts that participate, not just broadcast.
- **Comment on 20-30 posts/day** from target audience accounts. Genuine, specific comments — not "nice post." Reference what they actually said or showed.
- **Reply to every comment** on your own posts within the first hour. The algorithm weighs early engagement.
- **DM people who engage consistently.** Not a sales pitch — a conversation. "Where was that photo taken?"
- **Use DM triggers for conversion:** "DM us ISTANBUL and we'll send you our full city guide." 3-5x higher conversion than "link in bio."

### Content Rules
- No emojis in captions. Ever.
- Captions are short. 1-3 sentences max for feed posts. Save the depth for blog.
- Every caption should sound like something you'd text a well-traveled friend.
- Photos: natural light, warm tones, no HDR, no heavy filters. Film grain > Instagram filters.
- Reels: CapCut for editing. Trending audio when it fits the brand. No pointing-at-text trends.
- Carousels: DM Sans 800 for headlines, EB Garamond for accent. Dark backgrounds. Terracotta accents.

### What We Post vs. What We Don't

**Post:**
- Specific restaurant recommendations with details
- City neighborhoods worth exploring
- Travel behavior that signals taste (packing light, leaving the airport on a layover)
- Behind-the-scenes of building Rihlah
- Beautiful travel photography (ours, not stock)
- Member stories and travel profiles (Phase 2)

**Don't post:**
- Generic travel quotes over sunset photos
- "Top 10" listicles
- Product feature announcements that read like release notes
- Anything that starts with "We're so excited to announce..."
- Overtly religious content or Quran verses as captions
- Anything you'd see on a halal travel blog from 2019

## Reels / Video Strategy

Short-form video is the growth engine. Instagram's algorithm favors Reels for reach — this is how new people find Rihlah.

### Content Types That Work
1. **City walks** — phone in hand, walking through a neighborhood. Trending audio. Text overlay with neighborhood name + 1-2 tips. (15-30 seconds)
2. **Food close-ups** — the moment a dish arrives, street food being made, market produce. No talking needed. (10-20 seconds)
3. **Quick tips** — talking head or text-on-screen. "One thing nobody tells you about [city]." (15-30 seconds)
4. **The contrast** — "What people think [city] is like vs. what it's actually like." Side by side. (15-20 seconds)
5. **Founder POV** — Z talking directly about why Rihlah exists, a travel story, an observation. Authentic, unscripted energy. (30-60 seconds)

### Production
- **Camera:** iPhone. No professional equipment needed.
- **Editing:** CapCut (free). Auto-captions, transitions, speed ramps.
- **Audio:** trending sounds when they fit, original audio for talking head
- **Posting:** 2-3 Reels/week minimum. Consistency > quality for growth.

## Feature Marketing

Features should never be marketed as features. They should be marketed as moments.

| Feature | Don't say | Say |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| AI concierge | "Our AI-powered travel assistant" | "It's 3pm in Istanbul. Asr is at 5:15. You have 2 hours. Here's what to do near Sultanahmet." |
| Member profiles | "Browse traveler profiles" | "She's been to 14 countries. She's in Lisbon right now. You're going next month." |
| City guides | "Access our curated city guides" | "The Kadikoy guide that made me rethink Istanbul." |
| Ambient presence | "See who's traveling where" | "3 Rihlah members are in Barcelona this week. You didn't plan it. That's the point." |
| Application filter | "We're an exclusive community" | "Not everyone gets in. The people who do are worth knowing." |
| Trip intel | "Community-verified recommendations" | "A member posted this 3 days ago: 'Skip the Galata Tower. Go to Pierre Loti instead.' She was right." |

**The principle:** Show the experience, not the interface. Nobody cares about your UI. They care about the moment your product creates.

## Newsletter Strategy

### Platform: Beehiiv (free tier to start)

### Format: "The Brief" — weekly, every Thursday
Short, opinionated, useful. Not a blog digest. Original content that's worth opening.

**Structure:**
1. **The City** (200 words) — one city, one angle, one opinion. Not comprehensive — provocative.
2. **The Table** (100 words) — one restaurant or food experience. Specific enough to act on.
3. **The Detail** (50 words) — one practical tip. "Download Maps.me before you land in Morocco. Google Maps is useless in medinas."
4. **From the Community** (50 words) — one member quote, trip update, or connection story. (Phase 2)
5. **One link** — the blog post, the city guide, or the app.

**Growth mechanics:**
- "Share with 3 friends, get early access to [next city guide / AI concierge beta]"
- Beehiiv's referral program handles tracking
- Cross-promote on Instagram Stories every Thursday
- Bio link includes newsletter signup

### Name It
Not "The Rihlah Newsletter." Give it a name that feels like a publication. "The Brief" or "Departures" or "Rihlah Letters." Something you'd want to open.

### Welcome Sequence (automated, 3 emails)
When someone signs up for the waitlist or newsletter:
1. **Day 0:** "You're on the list." — Confirmation + what Rihlah is (2 sentences, not a manifesto)
2. **Day 3:** "While you wait." — Best carousel/blog post. Show the editorial quality.
3. **Day 7:** "The kind of people we're building this for." — Member profile or travel story. Reinforce identity.

When applications open: email the waitlist first. 48-hour early access. Rewards patience, creates urgency for the Instagram audience.

### When to Launch
Not yet. Launch the newsletter when Instagram hits 500 followers OR when you have 3 blog posts worth linking to. Whichever comes first. Email is the only channel you own. Instagram can change algorithms tomorrow. Your email list is yours forever.

## Community Seeding (First 100 Members)

The first 100 members define the community forever. Get this wrong and you're building on sand.

### The Playbook

**Step 1: Inner circle (members 1-20)**
People Z knows personally. Friends, friends-of-friends, people from the Muslim professional networks who travel well. They join because Z asks them to. They stay because the product is good.

- Personal DM or text. Not a mass invite.
- "I'm building something. I want you to be one of the first people in it."
- These people give honest feedback and set the culture.

**Step 2: Extended network (members 21-50)**
People who follow the Instagram, engage with posts, DM questions. The "I've been watching this" crowd.

- Soft CTA in Instagram Stories: "If you've been thinking about applying, now's the time."
- Application form is live. Review every application personally.
- Acceptance message feels personal, not automated.

**Step 3: Organic growth (members 51-100)**
Word of mouth from members 1-50. The referral loop kicks in.

- "Know someone who should be here? Send them the link."
- Each member can invite 2 people directly (skip the application).
- This creates social proof + accountability (you vouch for who you invite).

**Step 0: City-based density**
Don't seed everywhere. Pick 3 cities (London, Toronto, NYC) and get 20-30 members in each. Density in one city > 200 members scattered globally. 25 members in London who might actually cross paths creates real community. 200 members spread across 50 cities is a ghost town.

**Founding member status:**
First 200 members get "Founding Member" designation permanently. Free to implement, creates lasting loyalty. Founding members get early access to features + input on product direction + permanent free tier or discount when premium launches.

### What Members 1-100 Should Experience
- A profile that feels like a travel identity, not a dating profile
- At least 3 city guides worth reading
- The ability to see who else is in the community and where they're going
- A DM conversation with at least one person they didn't know before
- The feeling of "these are my people"

## Partnerships (Phase 2-3)

Not yet. But here's the playbook for when the audience is there.

### Tourism Boards
**What we offer:** Access to a curated, high-intent Muslim traveler audience that no other platform can reach.
**What we ask:** Sponsored content deals ($5-15K), press trips, exclusive access for members.
**When to approach:** 2,000+ Instagram followers OR 500+ app members.
**First targets:** Turkish Tourism Board, Visit Sarajevo, Morocco (ONMT), Uzbekistan Tourism, Visit Jordan.

### Boutique Hotels / Riads
**What we offer:** Featured placement in city guides, newsletter mentions, member-exclusive rates.
**What we ask:** Affiliate commission (10-15%) or flat sponsorship fee.
**When to approach:** When the relevant city guide has meaningful traffic.

### Trip Organizers
**What we offer:** Distribution. Their trips posted to an audience that's ready to book.
**What we ask:** 10-15% commission on bookings through Rihlah.
**When to approach:** Phase 3 — when the community has enough density to fill trips.

### Travel Creators / Micro-Influencers
**What we offer:** Feature on Rihlah's platform, early access to community.
**What we ask:** Cross-promotion, content collaboration, takeovers.
**When to approach:** Now. Start building relationships before you need them.
**Who to target:** Muslim travel creators with 5K-50K followers who have good taste and align with the brand. Not the ones with generic "halal travel" content.

## Metrics That Matter

### Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Brand
- Instagram followers (target: 500 by end of Month 2)
- Post saves and shares (more important than likes)
- Website visits from Instagram
- Newsletter signups
- DMs from people asking "how do I join?"

### Phase 2 (Month 3-6): Community
- App applications submitted
- Application-to-member conversion rate
- Active members (logged in within 7 days)
- Connections made between members
- City guide views

### Phase 3 (Month 6-12): Revenue
- Free-to-paid conversion rate (target: 10%+)
- Newsletter open rate (target: 40%+)
- Affiliate click-through rate
- Partnership deal pipeline
- Member referral rate

### Kill Signals
- < 200 followers after 60 days of consistent posting -> rethink content strategy
- < 2% engagement rate -> content isn't resonating, adjust pillars
- < 50 app applications after 90 days -> audience isn't converting, audit the funnel
- Members joining but not connecting with each other -> product problem, not marketing problem

## The Principle

Marketing is not separate from the product. Every piece of content IS the product in Phase 1. The Instagram is Rihlah. The blog is Rihlah. The newsletter is Rihlah. The app is just where the community lives once people are ready.

Don't market the app. Market the feeling of being around the right people in the right cities. The app sells itself once someone wants in.
