Market Size

  • Global halal tourism market: $320B (2026), projected $579B by 2036 (6.1% CAGR)
  • Muslim international travel arrivals: 186M (2025), projected 245M by 2030
  • Total Muslim travel spending projected: $230B by 2030
  • Gen Y + Gen Z identified as fastest-growing segment
Key Insight: The biggest Muslim travel businesses sell infrastructure (HalalBooking) or ads (HHWT). Nobody is building a premium community + AI product for Western Muslim travelers. The gap is not technology or content — it's brand and community.

Adjacent Comps (non-Muslim)

  • Raya — application-based social network. ~$10M ARR. Tiny team. Proves curated community can charge subscription.
  • Soho House — membership community. $1B+ revenue. Physical + digital. Proves membership model at scale.
  • Flash Pack — $32M. Proves curated group travel for a specific demographic works.
  • Luxus Travel — $1.6M year one. Proves "Pied Piper" community leader model for group travel.
