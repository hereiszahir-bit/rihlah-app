Rihlah — Legal Checklist

Everything needed before collecting money or launching on app stores.

Item Status Due Date Notes Est. Cost
Entity + Banking
LLC formation Not started Before launch Delaware or Wyoming recommended for tech. Can do online in 1-2 days. $100-500
EIN (Employer Identification Number) Not started After LLC Free from IRS. Needed for bank account and Stripe. Free
Business bank account Not started After EIN Mercury or Relay recommended for startups. No minimum balance. Free
Stripe account Not started After bank account Connect to business bank. Handles subscriptions + marketplace payouts. Free (2.9% + $0.30/txn)
Legal Documents
Terms of Service Not started Before launch Covers: membership, subscriptions, refund policy, user conduct, content ownership, limitation of liability. $0-500
Privacy Policy Not started Before launch Required for App Store + Play Store. Must cover data collection, usage, third-party sharing. GDPR if EU users. $0-500
Cookie consent / GDPR Not started Before launch Required if any EU members. Cookiebot or similar for website. In-app consent flow. Free
Data processing agreement Not started If processing EU data Required under GDPR if you process EU resident data. Template available. Free (template)
App Stores
Apple Developer account Not started Before iOS launch Enroll as organization (requires D-U-N-S number for LLC). $99/year
Google Play developer account Not started Before Android launch One-time registration fee. $25 one-time
IP Protection
Trademark search — "Rihlah" Not started Before launch Check availability in Class 42 (software) and Class 39 (travel services). $0-400
Trademark registration Not started After validation USPTO. File in relevant classes. Takes 8-12 months to process. $250-350/class
Domain ownership confirmed Not started Done rihlah.io — confirm registrar, auto-renew enabled, WHOIS privacy. ~$40/year
Insurance
General liability insurance Not started Before revenue Basic business liability. NOT travel operator insurance (we don't operate trips). $500-1,000/year
Status key: Not started In progress Done

Total estimated startup legal costs: $1,200-3,500. Review monthly until all items are complete.
