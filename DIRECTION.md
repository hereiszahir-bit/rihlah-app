# Rihlah — Strategic Direction

> This document is the source of truth for what Rihlah is, who it's for, and how it makes money.
> Read this before every session. Do not deviate without updating this document first.

---

## What Rihlah Is

A private travel community for Muslims who move with intention.

Technology-first. Community-driven. Operationally lean. No trips to operate. No logistics to manage.

The product is the network — the members, the intelligence, the connections. Content builds the audience, the app creates the community, AI powers the travel intelligence, and the network generates revenue.

**One-liner:** Travel with your people.

---

## Who It's For

25-35 year old Muslims in Western cities (London, Toronto, NYC, DC, Houston, Sydney).

They travel 2-4x/year. They care about their faith — but they're not looking for an "Islamic travel experience." They want to travel well and not compromise. Halal food without it being the whole personality. Prayer times without it being an app about prayer times.

They follow ALD, drink specialty coffee, have opinions about architecture. They think most "Muslim apps" look cheap and feel preachy. They want a brand that gets them.

**Rihlah is a cultural product, not a religious product.** Built by a Muslim, for Muslims, informed by Islamic values — but not an Islamic service. No fatwas, no halal certification, no fiqh positions. Cultural comfort, not religious compliance.

## The Product

### Core Screens
1. **Discover** — curated city content in story format. Save what interests you. Daily engagement.
2. **Travelers** — browse members by destination, city, interest. Ambient. No pressure.
3. **City Guide** (activated when traveling) — AI concierge: prayer-aware itineraries, verified dining, local knowledge.
4. **Messages** — DMs between connected members.
5. **Profile** — travel identity: where you've been, where you want to go, travel style.

### Key Mechanics
- **Application to join** — not exclusive, but intentional. Short answer: "What does travel mean to you?"
- **Ambient presence** — "Going to Istanbul in March" / "Here now in Marrakech." No pressure to meet. Just presence.
- **Story-format content** — full-screen, swipeable city guides. The UI disappears. You just see content.
- **AI concierge** — "I'm in Istanbul, it's 3pm, Asr is at 5:15. What should I do near Sultanahmet?" No other app does this.
- **Community-verified data** — members verify restaurants, mosques, experiences. Better than anonymous reviews.

### Design Principles
- **Dark-first.** Light apps feel utilitarian. Dark apps feel intimate.
- **Full-bleed imagery.** Photos edge to edge. No card borders everywhere.
- **Story-like design.** Every screen feels like a page turning, not a dashboard.
- **Typography as design.** Big serif headlines, tight spacing. Text is visual.
- **Negative space.** Premium = breathing room.

## Brand Identity

### Voice
- Confident, quiet, editorial
- "We went" not "You should go"
- Reference Islam naturally, never make it the headline
- Show taste, don't explain taste
- No emojis. Ever.

### Design System
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display (serif, headers) + Inter (sans, body)
- **App palette:** Dark-first — charcoal background, warm accents
- **Website palette:** Warm sand #f5f1ea, Stone #1c1917, Terracotta #b87a56, Olive #4a5d3e, Aged gold #c4956a
- **Photography:** Natural light, golden hour, people mid-moment (not posed), muted warm edit, film grain not HDR
- **Logo:** Wordmark only. "RIHLAH" in clean serif. No icons, no crescents.

### Content Pillars
1. **The City** — opinionated, deep destination content
2. **The Table** — food as connector
3. **The People** — real Rihlah members, their stories
4. **The Detail** — specific, practical, insider knowledge

### Platform Priority
1. Instagram (primary — reels + carousels, editorial aesthetic)
2. TikTok (secondary — repurpose reels)
3. rihlah.io (home base — brand + waitlist)
4. App (the product)
5. Email (conversion channel)

## Socio-Religious Positioning

### Principles
- **Cultural product, not religious product.** Islam is context, not content.
- **Don't claim halal. Don't claim Islamic. Claim Muslim.** "Muslim" is identity. "Halal" is certification. We're not inviting a standards debate.
- **The brand's tone is the filter.** The editorial voice attracts the middle and bores the extremes.
- **Show, don't declare.** The diversity, the comfort, the values — they show up in the community, not mission statements.

### Positions (implicit, never stated)
- Pan-Muslim. Start with destinations that resonate across the spectrum.
- Premium positioning is not apologized for.
- Mixed-gender community. The audience self-selects.

### What we never do
- Issue religious opinions or fatwas
- Certify anything as "halal" or "shariah-compliant"
- Engage with "haram police" or purity debates online
- Use crescent moons, green-and-gold, or "Islamic app" visual language
